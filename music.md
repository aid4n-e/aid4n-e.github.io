# Music

I'm a lifelong music lover, multi-instrumentalist, and composer. I've spent a decade producing as a hobby, and I'd be glad to take commissions for original media soundtracks.
<div></div>
I write all of my own music (minus samples) and don't believe that AI should be involved in the creative process. I also don't use template tracks, or royalty-free beats from the Internet.
