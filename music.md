# Music

I'm a lifelong music lover, multi-instrumentalist, and composer. I've spent a decade producing as a hobby, and I'd be glad to take commissions for original media soundtracks.
<div></div>
I write all of my own music (excluding samples, which are disclosed) and don't believe that AI should be involved in the creative process. I also don't use template tracks, or royalty-free beats from the Internet.

- I prefer to produce with FL Studio, though I am proficient with Ableton, Logic, and Reaper.
- My main instrument is the Hydrasynth Explorer. It has an excellent level of tonal flexibility and I enjoy the workflow of using it. 
- For drum and bass, I love the sound of the MODO DRUM and MODO BASS plugins.
- TONEX for guitar effects and amp sim.
- UA plugins for tape sim, echo, reverb.
